# Ethics Framework

The Carboncopies Foundation is committed to ensuring that the development of whole brain emulation proceeds responsibly, with rigorous attention to human dignity, scientific integrity, and global equity. Our Ethics Framework provides the structured principles that guide this work.

[Visit the Full Ethics Framework :octicons-arrow-right-24:](https://sites.google.com/carboncopies.org/ethics){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Why Ethics Matters for WBE

Whole brain emulation raises foundational questions that no other technology faces so directly: What constitutes personal identity? Does a functional emulation have moral status? Who bears responsibility for an emulated mind's wellbeing? These are not hypothetical edge cases; they are questions that must be answered before, not after, the technology matures.

The Ethics Framework is developed collaboratively with our [Consciousness & Subjective Experience](/Events/JournalClubs/ConsciousnessAndSubjectiveExperience) journal club and draws on expertise from philosophy of mind, bioethics, law, and neuroscience.

## Core Themes

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **:material-account-heart: Personal Identity & Continuity**

    ---

    What makes a person the same person over time, and does an emulation preserve that continuity? The framework addresses theories of psychological continuity, substrate independence, and the conditions under which an emulation could be considered the same individual.

-   **:material-scale-balance: Moral Status**

    ---

    If an emulation is functionally equivalent to a biological brain, does it have interests deserving moral consideration? The framework examines criteria for moral patienthood and how they apply to substrate-independent minds.

-   **:material-earth: Access & Global Justice**

    ---

    Advanced neurotechnology must not become an instrument of inequality. The framework addresses fair access, governance structures, and the responsibility to ensure WBE benefits humanity broadly rather than a privileged few.

-   **:material-shield-check: Safety & Oversight**

    ---

    Responsible development requires anticipating failure modes and establishing oversight mechanisms before they are needed. The framework outlines principles for safety review, transparency, and accountability in WBE research and deployment.

</div>
